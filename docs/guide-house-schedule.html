Guides / House schedule

Share a house schedule

Give many rooms one wake time and one bed time. Each room lighting controller follows the house schedule without wires, and an optional MQTT node lets Home Assistant change the times.

Before you wire it

A room lighting controller styles its lights by a wake time and a bed time (default 07:00 and 23:00). Levels rise to the daytime level over the hour after waking, stay at the daytime brightness and colour through the day, wind down to the sleep settings over the three hours before bed, and stay at the sleep settings until you wake. Without a schedule, every room keeps its own two times.

The house schedule (home-house-schedule) holds one wake and bed time for every room that uses it. It is a configuration node, like an MQTT broker: it has no wires and does not sit on the canvas. You choose it in each room controller’s Schedule setting, and it appears with the editor’s other configuration nodes.

  • You need at least one room lighting controller already wired to its lights.
  • Times are 24-hour HH:MM in the Node-RED host’s local time. Docker containers default to UTC: set the container’s TZ if the host should follow a local timezone.
  • The house schedule MQTT node is optional. Rooms follow the schedule without it; add it when you want the times on MQTT or in Home Assistant.
House schedule settings
SettingDefaultWhat it does
NamehouseLabels the schedule and names its topics, home/<name>/schedule/.... Needs a letter or digit. In topics the name is lower-cased and other characters become _: Main House publishes on home/main_house/schedule/....
Wake time07:00When the rooms start to brighten.
Bed time23:00When the rooms reach the sleep settings. It must differ from the wake time.

A bed time after midnight, or a wake time later than the bed time, works.

Share one schedule between rooms

Rooms reference the schedule; only the optional MQTT node has wires. Each room hears of every change as it happens.

What connects to what
FromConnect toWhy
Room controller Schedule settinghouse scheduleThe room takes its wake and bed times from it. No wire.
house schedule MQTT Schedule settinghouse scheduleRequired. The node publishes and changes this schedule. No wire.
MQTT inhouse schedule MQTT inputSubscribe to home/<name>/schedule/wake-time/set and bed-time/set.
house schedule MQTT outputMQTT outIts MQTT publications: the times, availability and Home Assistant discovery.
  1. Add the schedule from a room.

    Open a room lighting controller (home-room-lighting-controller) and set Schedule. Use the editor’s add button to create a house schedule, or pick one that already exists; the editor offers an existing schedule first for a new room.

  2. Set the times once.

    Edit the house schedule’s Wake time and Bed time. Every room that uses it follows these times, not its own Wake time and Bed time.

  3. Point the other rooms at it.

    Choose the same schedule as Schedule in each room that should share the times.

  4. Add the MQTT node if you want it.

    Drag a house schedule MQTT node (home-house-schedule-mqtt in the palette) onto the canvas, choose the schedule as its Schedule, and wire it to the broker as in the table. Use one per schedule: two would share the same topics and entities, and deleting one clears them for both until the other next starts.

The importable example House schedule.json shows two rooms sharing one schedule named house, with an Inject node that changes the wake time through the MQTT node.

Change the times

Send changes to the house schedule MQTT node. It changes the shared schedule, and every room following it restyles. Give new times as HH:MM, either one alone:

Function → house schedule MQTT · wake earlier
msg.lighting = {kind: "schedule", wakeTime: "06:30"};
return msg;
Function → house schedule MQTT · set both times
msg.lighting = {kind: "schedule", wakeTime: "06:30", bedTime: "22:30"};
return msg;

Times that are not valid HH:MM, or a wake time equal to the bed time, are ignored.

From MQTT, a time on a set topic does the same. A message is read as HH:MM or HH:MM:SS; retained messages and anything but a time are ignored.

House schedule topics (default name house)
TopicDirectionPayload
home/house/schedule/wake-time/setInA new wake time
home/house/schedule/bed-time/setInA new bed time
home/house/schedule/wake-time/stateOut, retainedThe wake time in use, HH:MM:SS
home/house/schedule/bed-time/stateOut, retainedThe bed time in use, HH:MM:SS
home/house/schedule/availabilityOut, retainedonline after the flows start; offline as the node stops (see below)

With Home Assistant discovery on (the editor’s default for a new node; an imported flow without the setting gets none), Home Assistant gets a Wake time and a Bed time entity under the Discovery prefix (homeassistant), named after the schedule so a replacement MQTT node keeps them. Changing one in Home Assistant sends it to the set topic, and the node acts on it. See Home Assistant for the rest of the setup.

The node’s own offline, and the clearing of its retained topics and entities when you delete it, reach the broker only on a “Modified nodes” or “Modified flows” deploy. On a full deploy, a restart or a crash, Node-RED stops the MQTT out node at the same moment, so set the MQTT broker node’s Birth message to online and its Close message and Will message to offline, all retained, on the availability topic. See Upgrading from 2.x in the README.

To announce the times again, as after the broker reconnects, send the node msg.source = "startup".

A changed time lasts until the setting changes.

A time set by a message or from Home Assistant stays until a deploy gives the house schedule a different Wake time or Bed time; that setting then applies. Without a file context store, a restart or redeploy also returns the schedule to its settings.

Give a room its own times

A room that needs different times (a child’s room, say) sets Schedule to none and keeps its own Wake time and Bed time.

Following a schedule or not
Room’s ScheduleIts timesIts own wake and bed time topics and entities
A house scheduleThe schedule’s, at every changeIts wake-time/set and bed-time/set topics are ignored; its Home Assistant wake and bed time entities are cleared.
NoneIts own settingsWith Publish state over MQTT on, published and changed under home/<area>/wake-time and bed-time, as in Room light control.

Sleep stays per room. A room’s sleep mode still overrides the schedule, so one room can go to its sleep settings while the others follow the house times.

Deleting a house schedule in the editor sets its rooms to none, and they return to their own times rather than keeping the schedule’s last ones.

Room status circadian or sleepThe room is styling its lights by the times in use.
Room status schedule missing: own timesThe room names a schedule that is not running, as when it was deleted from the flows file. It keeps its own times until you choose a schedule or none.
Retained home/<name>/schedule/wake-time/state and bed-time/stateThe MQTT node published the times; watch them with an MQTT client or a Debug node on its output.

A change restyles only the lights a room has been told about since it started. A light that nothing has named since a start with nothing saved can keep its start-time style; switch circadian lighting on, or let motion or a wall switch name it.
